          Fallen Sword Path Finder

          Browse every realm of Fallen Sword by level, see how they connect and what they hold (creatures, shops, quests, relics, portals), and find the shortest way from one area to another.

          How to use it

          • Use Route from / to in the header to get step-by-step directions. Stairs, portals, world maps and the 25 stamina teleport to Mountain Path are all considered.
          • Hover or tap a realm for its info card. Drag realms to rearrange them; positions are kept in your browser and Reset layout puts them back.
          • Press / to search for an area.

          Where the data comes from

          Collected from the Ultimate Fallen Sword Guide and the Fallen Sword Wiki. It is community-maintained, so some stairs may be missing or out of date. Route lengths are estimates: walking is the straight-line distance and obstacles are ignored.

          Feedback

          Suggestions and bug reports are welcome on GitHub; please open an issue there. Wrong or missing stairs are especially useful, with the realm names.
